Help test SMART trip for Android

SMART trip is already on the App Store for iPhone. Before Google lets us publish the Android app on Google Play, it has to be tested by real riders. If you have an Android phone, you can help.

You don't need to be on a train to test it. Checking times, planning a trip and opening the live map from home all count.

How to join

  1. Join the tester group with the Google account you use on your Android phone: SMART trip Android testers.
  2. Opt in to the test with the same account: become a tester.
  3. Install SMART trip from Google Play. It can take a few minutes after opting in before the install button appears.

SMART trip needs Android 7.0 or newer.

What we ask of testers

  • Stay opted in for at least 14 days. Google only counts testers who stay in the test for 14 days in a row.
  • Use it like you normally would: check departure times, plan a trip, open the live map, set a leave reminder, or share a trip.
  • Tell us what you think: what works, what's confusing, and what's broken.

Sending feedback

  • In the app, tap Send feedback on the tester card or at the bottom of the home screen. It fills in your app version and device for you.
  • Or email smart-train@proton.me.
  • Or leave private feedback on the app's Google Play page. Only the developer can see it.

Privacy

Joining the test shares your Google account email with the developer so Google Play can give you access. The app itself has no account and no analytics. See the Privacy Policy.

SMART trip is a community project and is not affiliated with Sonoma-Marin Area Rail Transit (SMART).
